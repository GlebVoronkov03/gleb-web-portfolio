PLER 2.0 — Peak Length to Error Ratio

Full-reference geometric fidelity for 3D assets.

GitHubPaper PDFBench

Problem

Classic mesh distances often miss perceptual geometric fidelity. Early PLER sampling was uneven across the surface.

Fibonacci (golden) spiral sampling was identified as optimal — PLER 2.0.

Interactive scheme

Hover nodes on the diagram. On the home page, drag the live Fibonacci sphere.

Results

  • Uniform sphere sampling study → Fibonacci spiral
  • Cache ~1.8–3.7× on repeat local runs
