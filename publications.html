2025

PLER: A Novel Full-Reference Metric for Evaluating the Geometric Fidelity of 3D Assets

Voronkov G.A., Balobanov A.V. · MTUCI Research Department “Digital Television and Video-informatics”

Enhancement of PLER (Peak Length to Error Ratio) for objective 3D quality assessment. The original sphere-ray design suffered from non-uniform ray distribution; Fibonacci (golden) spiral sampling yields PLER 2.0 with improved accuracy and stability on test meshes.
